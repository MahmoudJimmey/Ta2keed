# Security & data protection

Short version: **your shop's data stays in your own copy of Ta2keed** (your computer or your server). The repo and the Ta2keed authors never receive it. Secrets are encrypted, the dashboard is password protected, and **no deposit order ships until you confirm the money really arrived**.

## Where things are stored

| Data | Where | Protection |
|---|---|---|
| Orders, customers (name, phone, address), chats, payments, event log | `ta2keed.db` (SQLite) in the data folder: `instance/` locally, `/data` on a host | File permissions 0600, WAL journaling, `secure_delete` on |
| Receipt screenshots waiting for your confirmation | `<data>/receipts/` | 0600, served only to the logged-in owner |
| API keys and tokens (WhatsApp, Bosta, AI, Telegram) | `<data>/settings.json` | **Encrypted at rest** (Fernet: AES-128 + HMAC-SHA256) |
| Encryption key | `<data>/.master.key`, or better, the `TA2KEED_SECRET_KEY` env var on your host | Keep it outside the data disk on hosts |
| Owner password | `settings.json` | One-way hash (PBKDF2-SHA256, 120k rounds), never stored in plain text |
| Your shop profile (products, fees) | `<data>/store.json` | Not secret |

Nothing in the data folder is committed to git (`.gitignore` covers `instance/`, `.env`, `*.db`).

**Third parties that see data, and only when you connect them:**
- **Meta (WhatsApp):** the messages exchanged with customers.
- **Your AI provider (optional):** message text and receipt images, for understanding them.
- **Bosta:** name, phone, address and cash amount for each shipment.
- **Telegram:** your owner alerts and the receipt photos sent to you.

The "offline" mode uses no AI provider at all.

## Dashboard & API protection
- Owner password required when accessed from outside your computer. Sessions are signed, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Strict` and `Secure` on https, and last 30 days.
- A fresh online server can only be claimed with the one-time setup link printed in the server log.
- Login brute-force protection: 8 failed attempts lock that IP for 15 minutes. Failed logins are logged.
- Cross-site request protection: browser POSTs must come from Ta2keed's own pages.
- Security headers: CSP, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `nosniff`, HSTS on https, `no-store` on API responses.
- WhatsApp webhook signature verification (`X-Hub-Signature-256`) when the App secret is set, and a shared secret on courier webhooks.
- Request size cap of 12 MB.
- The AI never controls money. Prices, totals, deposits and shipments are deterministic code, so a prompt-injection test proves a customer can't talk the agent into a free order.

## Payment fraud: three layers
A receipt screenshot **by itself proves nothing**. It can be edited, generated by AI, or a real receipt of an **old** transfer. So Ta2keed uses three layers:

1. **Automatic checks** (instant): is it a receipt, was the transfer successful, is the amount ≥ the deposit, is the recipient *your* InstaPay / Vodafone Cash, and has this reference number or exact image **ever been used before** (including receipts still waiting for confirmation)? With an AI vision model it also flags edited-looking images.
2. **Owner confirmation** (default ON): the screenshot and details are sent to **you** on Telegram (✅ / ❌ buttons) and/or WhatsApp (reply buttons), and shown on the dashboard. You check your bank or InstaPay app and tap **Received** or **Not received**. **The order is held until you do.** Reminders go out after 30 min and 3 h. Only your own Telegram chat, your WhatsApp number or your logged-in dashboard can decide.
3. **The customer's history:** a rejected receipt is recorded as fraud, and refusals feed the risk score, so repeat offenders always need a deposit.

You can turn layer 2 off in **Settings → Your shop**, but it's recommended.

## Your customers' data
- **Export** everything stored about one customer: `GET /api/admin/customer/<phone>`.
- **Erase** a customer (e.g. on request): `DELETE /api/admin/customer/<phone>`. It deletes their chats and history and anonymises their orders (totals are kept for your accounting).
- **Backup** the whole shop: `GET /api/admin/backup`, a zip of the database, shop profile and encrypted settings. The encryption key is deliberately *not* included, so keep it separately.

## Running it without your computer
See [deploy.md](deploy.md). On Render or any Docker host, set:
- `TA2KEED_DATA_DIR=/data` on a **persistent disk**
- `ADMIN_PASSWORD` (or claim with the one-time link)
- `TA2KEED_SECRET_KEY` (a long random string, stored in the host's secret settings rather than on the disk)
- `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET` and `COURIER_WEBHOOK_SECRET`

Keep regular backups (`/api/admin/backup`). Render disks also have daily snapshots.
